# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router with TypeScript, deployed on Vercel. Supabase stores waitlist leads; Resend delivers organizer notifications.

## Users

The primary audience is Hyderabad-based groups, broadly aged 20–40, whose school, college, hostel, neighbourhood, or first-job friendships now mostly live in group chats. Older reunion communities remain welcome and visible.

## Product Purpose

Reunion is a consumer event landing page for “The Festival of Friendship.” Its first release creates awareness and turns interest into crew-aware email waitlist registrations ahead of the December 2026 event.

## Positioning

Most festivals are built around the stage. Reunion is built around the people visitors arrive with: their crew is the headliner, and music, games, discovery, food, flea, and shared memories give them reasons to come together.

## Operating Context

Visitors discover the event through social content, alumni communities, and group chats, then visit primarily on mobile. They need to understand the proposition quickly, share it with friends, and join with lightweight details about their crew.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public event timing is “December 2026”; the exact date is not confirmed and must not appear.
- Boulder Hills, Hyderabad and 4 PM–10 PM are treated as confirmed.
- The primary action is joining the email waitlist. Phone and WhatsApp data are not collected.
- Waitlist data includes identity, email consent, crew size, affiliation, and campaign attribution.
- Artist names, ticket prices, attendance claims, purchase flows, invitations, leaderboards, sponsor material, and community dashboards are outside the first release.
- Exact artist and programming details in the source deck are not public until separately confirmed.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Reunion.
- Descriptor: The Festival of Friendship.
- Core lines: “Your people are the headliner” and “Don’t just remember the good times. Relive them.”
- The website evolves the sponsorship deck’s deep-purple, cream, amber, coral, magenta, and violet identity with condensed display typography and photographic warmth.
- Voice is direct, warm, culturally specific, and group-first; it never fabricates social proof or commercial claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- `REUNION.pdf` is the approved source for positioning, audience, event categories, brand language, and visual cues.
- Rendered deck pages are available under `tmp/pdfs/reunion/` for visual reference.
- No real testimonials, historic attendee figures, public ticket prices, confirmed artist lineup, or prior-event photography are available and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make friendship—not spectacle—the reason to act.
- Show the idea through crews, shared history, and reunion rituals rather than generic festival language.
- Keep pre-launch claims honest and visibly distinguish confirmed facts from coming-soon details.
- Minimize signup friction while retaining the group context that makes Reunion distinct.
- Earn trust through accessible, fast, mobile-first execution and explicit consent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. The page must support keyboard navigation, reduced motion, screen readers, 44px touch targets, high contrast, and a complete non-animated reading path.
