# Campaign image provenance

All five images were generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 15 October 2026 for this project. No image represents a past Reunion event or a confirmed attendee.

- `reunion-hero.png`: cinematic editorial photograph of six Indian friends reuniting at an outdoor Hyderabad evening festival; deep aubergine, amber, coral and magenta; no text or logos.
- `then-now.png`: matched photorealistic diptych of the same five Indian friends in college in 2010 and reunited in 2026; identical order and pose; no text or labels. `then-now.webp` is the browser-optimized derivative; the PNG remains the embedded-prompt master.
- `experience-games.png`: friends cheering through an outdoor mini-cricket target challenge at a Hyderabad festival; no text or logos.
- `experience-hunt.png`: friends solving a clue together in a Hyderabad-inspired festival market at dusk; no readable text or logos.
- `experience-flea.png`: friends sharing food and conversation in an evening Hyderabad-inspired flea market; no text, logos or alcohol emphasis.
